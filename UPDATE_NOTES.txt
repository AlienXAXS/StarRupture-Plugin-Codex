- Right-click an item in the game's recipe station, corporation terminal or machine menus to pin the recipe that makes it
- Ctrl+right-click the same item to open it in the Planner
- Pinned recipes are saved and come back next time you play
- Pins can be tiled or cascaded from any screen edge, dragged around, and stay on screen at any resolution
- Click the X on a pin's title bar to unpin it; pins can be moved and unpinned while the Esc menu or the Codex is open, or the cursor is up in a game menu
- Search with the Up and Down keys and Enter, and drag the search box where you like
- Planner button opens the item in the StarRupture Planner
- Needs ModLoader v1.23.0 or later
